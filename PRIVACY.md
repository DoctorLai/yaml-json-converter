# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-12_

**YAML ↔ JSON Converter** is designed to be private by default. This document
explains exactly what the application does and does not do with your data.

## The Short Version

- The app runs **entirely in your browser**. There is no backend server.
- Your YAML/JSON input is **never uploaded, transmitted, or stored remotely**.
- We do **not** use cookies, analytics, trackers, or advertising.
- We do **not** collect any personal information.

## Data You Enter

Anything you type or paste into the editors is processed locally in your browser
to perform the conversion. For convenience, the current contents of the two
editors are also saved to your browser's `localStorage` so they are restored when
you reload the page. This data never leaves your device and is never sent over
the network — use the **Clear** button to remove it at any time.

## Local Storage

For convenience, the app stores two small preferences in your browser's
`localStorage`:

| Key         | Purpose                                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `darkMode`  | Remembers your light/dark theme choice.     |
| `language`  | Remembers your selected interface language. |
| `yamlInput` | Remembers the contents of the YAML editor.  |
| `jsonInput` | Remembers the contents of the JSON editor.  |

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time via your
browser settings.

## Hosting

The public version is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. As with any web host, GitHub
may collect standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) when the page is
served. Please refer to
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for details. This is outside the control of this application.

## Third-Party Links

The footer contains links to external sites (for example, GitHub and Buy Me a
Coffee). Following those links is subject to the privacy policies of those
respective services.
